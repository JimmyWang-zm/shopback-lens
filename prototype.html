Turning every link, screenshot and question into a tracked order

Click any frame to open it full size and step through the flow with the arrow keys. Every screen here is also available as an SVG in /frames, which imports into Figma as editable vector layers and live text. Share-sheet capture is the new surface. Ask and Watch reuse capabilities ShopBack already ships.

Fidelity note. These are product-design frames, not a visual-identity exercise. Colour and type follow ShopBack's existing palette closely enough to read as ShopBack, but nothing here should be taken as final visual design. Product imagery is deliberately abstract rather than fake photography, so that attention stays on the pricing logic and the trust mechanics, which are the parts that carry risk.
