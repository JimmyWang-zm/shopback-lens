Product proposal · 12-month horizon

The cheapest incremental GMV is demand ShopBack already influences but fails to capture.

ShopBack already has an answer engine. What it does not have is an intercept for a product seen in TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp. Lens is that intercept. Smart Rates is how merchants buy verified incrementality without cutting anyone’s published rate.

The four questions

What. Social-first deal seekers in SG. Share a TikTok link or screenshot; get True Net Price; one-tap tracked checkout.

Why this. Capture problem, not a demand problem — and the gap in the live AI stack.

Assumptions. Every number is tagged [A-n] with a test and a kill condition. I do not have internal data.

Impact. Recover leaked GMV, Watch-driven repurchase, 71 bps platform fee on participating GMV.

What already exists. AI Assistant, Search by Photo, Smart Alerts, Travel Planner and the ChatGPT app all answer once the user is inside ShopBack. Lens is Share → ShopBack from the app they are already in. Ask and Watch are components of that surface, not a rebuild.
